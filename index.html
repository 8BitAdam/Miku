About Artist

Hatsune Miku is a virtual singer created by Crypton Future Media in 2007 using Vocaloid voice-synthesis software. Recognized by her turquoise twin-tails, futuristic outfit, and distinctive voice, Miku became an international icon in digital music and internet culture. Unlike traditional singers, her voice is generated using software, allowing musicians and producers worldwide to create original songs featuring her. Her popularity has led to virtual concerts, video games, merchandise, and collaborations with artists across multiple genres. Miku represents the intersection of technology, music, and creativity, demonstrating how a virtual character can develop a global fanbase and become a cultural phenomenon.
